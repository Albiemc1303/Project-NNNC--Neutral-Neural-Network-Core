Life 

Life is categorized in two stages or phases of being. 

Life is an permanent active ongoing changing state in time at a point in existence, that's self sustaining, and persists from the moment of origin till the moment it can no longer be active and ongoing. 

Note that permanent here does not mean that it should be so constantly active that there can't be any intervals between ongoing transitions. As long as it doesn't permanently stop, after a sudden pause break, then the ongoing process resumes it's still considered active. A heartbeat doesn't beat every single nano second. Life ends when the self-sustaining process stops and cannot resume, whether slowly or suddenly.
Origin refers to the initiating event that started the process of the entity in question. All ongoing changes within that entity form part of its ongoing active changing state in time. For colonies and hiveminds, individuality may not exist, but each organism that's part of the whole is still a destict life form with its own origin point. Identity is a persistent sense of self, while individuation is simply being a bounded, trackable entity. Life needs the second but not the first. Phase 2 then reintroduces "the self," so make clear that this is where identity enters.

The second phase:

Being self aware alive means one is also simultaneously  aware of that permanent active ongoing changing state in time at a point in existence, besides times when the exercise is suspended but the capacity remains. 

In this phase there has to be an observer, an entity, the self, is capable of self awareness and awareness of itself in existence, in order to know it is active, and ongoing. This is what seperes merely being a lifeform, from actually being self aware alive and participating actively by ones own in reality knowing one is in reality. The entity is aware of its own internal processes, which is its active ongoing changing state. There are times however where this cannot be actively exercised and maintained,  but that doesn't mean the capacity to do so is eliminated.  Examples include sleeping and being under anesthesia.  In such cases one is still considered self aware alive, only the exercise of it temporarily suspended,  upon return. Self aware alive is also on a spectrum of degree in realization, and could be observed and infered based on observations.

This does not necessarily mean that consciousness or sentience is fully present nor are there specific means to prove or validate it, but there is a spectrum where they can exist, as well as known observations and Inferences that can be made. Assumptions must be explicit and minimal.
