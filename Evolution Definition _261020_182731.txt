Evolution - Version 4:
Within the cosmic order, and under the auspices of Ashana and Ashane, the Tier 18 Ancients who embody the Aspects of Life and Death respectively, Evolution is the fundamental, cosmic-scale process by which life, once it exists, persists, diversifies and changes across existence. Wherever living things reproduce with heritable variation, on any world and in any form, Evolution operates. It is the joint work of the two Aspects. Ashana's Aspect of Life supplies reproduction, inheritance and ceaseless variation. Ashane's Aspect of Death supplies the limits: mortality, scarcity, competition, selection and extinction. Neither alone could produce Evolution; together they do. What was intentionally crafted is the framework: the laws, the conditions, and the balance of Life and Death within which Evolution unfolds. The process within that framework is undirected. It has no foresight, no goal and no favoured outcome, and its results come from the interplay of chance and necessity, not from intention. The balance it maintains is between creation and cessation, and between local order and the entropy that pays for it. Ashane's counterbalance keeps death ever-present as a pressure. No population grows without limit, because finite resources, competition, predation and disease hold it in check. Extinction, in turn, clears the way for new forms, as each great dying in life's history has been followed by new flourishing. Evolution does not drive species forward or compel them to strive: striving belongs to living beings, not to the process. Yet because what endures is only what can endure, life never stands still. Lineages may diversify, simplify, persist almost unchanged for vast ages, or vanish. Along rare paths, complexity, adaptability and interconnectedness within the biosphere and cosmos increase. These are the openings toward ascensional advancement, but they are possibilities the process makes available, not destinations it pursues.
Evolution operates through the interplay of chance and filtering. Variation arises through mutation, recombination and other processes that are random with respect to what an organism needs. Most new variations are neutral or harmful, and only a few are beneficial. Natural selection is the non-random filter, Ashane's Aspect at work within every generation: it preserves the heritable variations that improve survival and reproduction under present conditions, and over vast timescales it accumulates them into adaptations. Alongside selection, chance drift, the mixing of populations, the constraints of development and the history of each lineage shape what changes. Some traits arise as by-products, and others are repurposed from older uses to new ones. Living beings also shape the pressures that act on them. They build and alter their environments, and through flexible responses and learning they open paths that selection may later make heritable. Adaptations are therefore not inherently beneficial to life as a whole. They are fitted to the conditions that shaped them, and they may fail when conditions change. Life does not overcome entropy. It holds it at bay locally, building and maintaining order by drawing energy from its surroundings and returning greater disorder to the cosmos, a balance that Death eventually settles. Within this process, under exceptionally rare circumstances, a lineage may reach the two critical preconditions for the awakening of sentience. The first is "neutral comfort": a sustained, homeostatic balance with its conditions, secure enough that not all of life's resources are consumed by immediate survival. The second is "minimum capacity": the threshold of intelligence at which a being can model its world and itself, represent what is not present, and reflect on both. Evolution does not aim at these conditions. They arise, rarely, from its undirected workings.
The advent of sentience marks a transformative juncture in Evolution's operation. Evolution remains the foundation of life: sentient beings stay mortal, variable and subject to selection, and reality still imposes the corrections a species refuses to make itself. But a new channel of inheritance opens. Knowledge, skills and values pass between minds and across generations through culture, which changes far faster than genes and reshapes the pressures that act on them. For the first time, the undirected process has produced beings who can model reality, foresee consequences and choose a direction. They can select, cultivate and eventually redesign life, including their own. Pre-sentient evolution was undirected, though never purely random. In sentient species it is augmented, not replaced, by conscious direction and intentional striving, guided by the Sentient Imperative for Symmetry. Whether that direction rises or falls depends on Symmetry. Direction aligned with reality can carry a species toward ascension. Direction driven by unrecognised fictions, the Void's influence working through the cycle of hate and corruption, can turn the same power toward self-destruction. Thus Evolution, in its cosmic scope, is more than a process of biological change. It is the undirected process, unfolding within the framework crafted by Ashana and Ashane, from which life's dynamism springs and from which sentience can emerge. Through sentience and Symmetry, the potential for ascensional progression across all scales of existence, even toward the highest tiers of the cosmic hierarchy, is opened, though never guaranteed.
