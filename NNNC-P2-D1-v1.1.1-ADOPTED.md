# 0. Constitution, authority, scope, and interpretation

## 0.1 Purpose

This specification defines how the NNNC architecture distinguishes numerical individual identity, constitutive organizational continuation, informational equivalence, causal ancestry, lifecycle governance, and evidence-relative adjudication across time. It governs ordinary operation, dormancy, development, migration, branching, complete cold suspension, reactivation, termination, restoration, and succession.

The specification establishes an explicit **rule-relative architectural convention** for recognizing identity preservation. It does not purport to discover a metaphysically intrinsic identity marker in a checkpoint, state vector, physical substrate, or computation.

## 0.2 Normative force

**SHALL** and **SHALL NOT** are mandatory; **SHOULD** permits a documented exception; **MAY** grants a permitted option. A definition supplies the meaning of a normative term. An adjudication result describes a conclusion under an applicable rule and evidence; it is not itself an historical event.

## 0.3 Inherited constraints

The following accepted architectural distinctions remain controlling:

1. The NNNC entity is distinct from its Neural Execution Substrate (NES), including any particular resource, process, state representation, or implementation.
2. Active existential continuity is governed by the Phase 0 existential-heartbeat contract. No second heartbeat or fictional active computation is introduced for cold suspension.
3. Phase 1 organizational constitution, qualification, governance, custody, constituent boundaries, and continuity evidence remain applicable.
4. Phase 1 REQ-P1-039 governs the required cold-suspension custody evidence; custody and authorization do not alone prove identity.
5. Definitive termination followed by restoration is successor restoration, not preservation of the terminated individual.
6. Identity findings do not substitute for protected-write, ADMIN, authentication, delegation, fencing, or other authority checks.
7. The Five Flow Neural Network (forward, reverse, intermediate, subconscious, meta) and the six Systemic Algorithms (Intelligence, Reasoning, Critical Thinking, Symmetry, Evolution, Life) remain Phase 1 architectural commitments; Phase 2 does not redefine them or use their mere presence as identity proof.

## 0.4 Explicit boundaries

This document does not implement durable journals, recovery protocols, physical-effect idempotency, distributed ordering, cryptographic provenance, protected transitions, executable identity verification, or proof of consciousness. These are assigned to Document 2 and later implementation/verification phases under tracked obligations.

## 0.5 Interpretive priority and consolidation rule

The **Final Constitutive-Admission Architectural Decision** controls any inconsistent v0.3.4 wording. In particular, v0.3.4 Condition C's independent primitive-execution/interface-membership test and any separate event-admission primitive are **rejected**. The six-condition rule in §5.5 is exhaustive. Earlier provisions survive only as restated or as compatible background; this document is intended to be read independently, not as an amendment stack.

# 1. Identity ontology and non-substitution

**DEF-P2-001 — Numerical Individual Identity.** Numerical individual identity is the relation under which observations or organizational realizations at different temporal conditions refer to one and the same NNNC individual, rather than two individuals that merely resemble one another or share ancestry. Within a fixed interpretation, numerical identity is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. It is not a verdict, identifier, memory, or permission.

**DEF-P2-002 — Organizational Identity.** Organizational identity describes persistence of the NNNC's constitutive roles, relationships, integration, and admissible transformation history. It is relevant to, but not alone sufficient for, numerical identity.

**DEF-P2-003 — Informational Equivalence.** Two states or realizations are informationally equivalent relative to an explicitly stated comparison scope when their compared information is equivalent. Informational equivalence neither entails numerical identity nor creates a continuation right.

**DEF-P2-004 — Diachronic Continuity.** Diachronic continuity concerns an individual's persistence through successive temporal conditions under the applicable rule, including admissible changes in state, constituents, or activity.

**DEF-P2-005 — Snapshot-Derived Instance.** A snapshot-derived instance is a realization initialized using retained information from another instance. Its informational ancestry does not alone establish continuation.

**REQ-P2-001 — Stable historical referents.** Each continuity-relevant instance SHALL have a stable historical referent suitable for linking evidence, events, and verdicts. Referents SHALL NOT be confused with individuals. Identifier reuse, duplication, or renaming does not establish or terminate identity.

**REQ-P2-002 — Non-circularity.** Every predicate used to establish preservation SHALL be characterizable without assuming the preservation verdict. A claimant's self-description, an identity label, or an earlier unverified conclusion SHALL NOT constitute its own proof.

**REQ-P2-003 — Continuity evaluation.** The adjudicator SHALL evaluate the predecessor and candidate, constitutive organization, actual transitions, causal and lifecycle history, custody, role-critical dependencies, competing pathways, governing rule version, evidence provenance, and material uncertainty. No single factor is sufficient unless an applicable rule expressly establishes a disqualifying boundary.

**REQ-P2-004 — Non-equivalence.** Operational continuity, state equivalence, authorization, heartbeat activity, organizational similarity, and causal lineage SHALL NOT independently imply numerical identity. Their inclusion as components of a complete rule is not prohibited.

**REQ-P2-005 — Evidence preservation.** Continuity-relevant evidence SHALL be preserved under applicable custody and governance contracts. Mutable autobiography or evidence solely controlled by a claimant SHALL NOT independently establish identity. Missing or contradictory evidence SHALL be disclosed.

**REQ-P2-006 — State/entity separation.** State change does not necessarily change the individual; reproduction of state does not necessarily reproduce the individual.

**REQ-P2-007 — Distinct instances.** Independently operating realizations derived from common retained state SHALL remain distinguishable, with shared pre-divergence history and separately represented subsequent histories.

# 2. Constitutive organization, development, and active continuation

**DEF-P2-012 — Developmental Transformation.** Developmental transformation is change in NNNC state, capabilities, constituent organization, dependencies, or mechanisms through learning, adaptation, repair, growth, restructuring, or intervention.

**DEF-P2-013 — External Intervention.** An external intervention is an alteration initiated or imposed outside the evaluated NNNC's relevant internal organization, including repair, replacement, migration, recovery, and administration.

**DEF-P2-017 — Identity-Neutral Continuation Evidence.** Identity-neutral evidence comprises independently characterizable historical facts about organizational state, actual transitions, causal dependencies, heartbeat operation during active intervals, custody, lineage, branching, termination, and integration. “Neutral” means the facts do not presuppose the identity verdict; they need not be causally independent of the NNNC.

**DEF-P2-018 — Admissible Continuation History.** An admissible continuation history is a historically evaluable sequence of organizational and lifecycle events connecting predecessor and candidate under applicable contracts. Historical admissibility alone does not imply identity preservation.

**DEF-P2-020 — Constitutive Organizational Continuation.** Constitutive organizational continuation is a historical relation in which an existing organizational realization undergoes causally connected, lifecycle-admitted changes retaining required integration of its constitutive roles and dependencies, or passes through a qualifying nonterminal cold-suspension/release history under DEF-P2-022. It SHALL satisfy:

- **A — Historical dependence:** actual organizational transformation or qualified nonterminal release, not merely informational derivation.
- **B — Organizational integration:** required constitutive roles and dependencies remain integrated through active transitions; particular physical constituents may change.
- **C — Nonterminal passage:** applicable independently defined active or suspension lifecycle conditions are satisfied.
- **D — Distinct initialization:** independently initialized descendants do not qualify through similarity, state copying, or retrospective designation.
- **E — Independence:** neither identity verdict, governance designation, authorization alone, matching identifiers, copied state, nor absence of rivals defines continuation.
- **F — Non-sufficiency:** constitutive continuation is necessary but not alone sufficient for `PRESERVED`.
- **G — Historical organizational dependence:** the referent is the actually realized organization and its history, not a favorable identity verdict. Active integration is not required during valid cold suspension; §5 supplies the alternate constitutive convention.

**DEF-P2-021 — Independent Informational Reconstruction.** An independent informational reconstruction is a distinct organizational realization initiated from retained or reproduced information outside an established active constitutive continuation and outside an effective qualifying release from the predecessor's suspended lifecycle locus. The classification rests on actual origin, not whether information is complete, the operation is authorized, the source is inactive, the same NES is used, or the descendant operates first. If continuation versus reconstruction cannot be established or excluded, the classification is unresolved; uncertainty SHALL NOT be coerced into reconstruction.

**REQ-P2-018 — Identity-compatible development.** Identity evaluation SHALL permit genuine change without requiring immutable memories, parameters, resources, modules, or constituent membership.

**REQ-P2-019 — Cumulative cohesion.** Cumulative transformation SHALL be evaluated in addition to local changes, considering role-criticality, integration, causal history, rates and magnitudes of change, and actual identity-disrupting boundaries. Locally admissible steps do not automatically prove global continuity. An established numerical-identity relation remains transitive; new evidence may instead reveal an earlier mistaken verdict.

**REQ-P2-020 — Threshold discipline.** No universal percentage of constituent replacement determines identity. Later thresholds require stated scope, assumptions, and justification.

**REQ-P2-021 — Authorization separation.** Authorized interventions are not automatically identity-preserving; unauthorized interventions are not automatically identity-terminating. Governance violations remain separately actionable.

**REQ-P2-022 — Substrate migration.** Moving an NNNC between NES resources neither preserves nor terminates identity automatically. Active integrated migration must satisfy DEF-P2-020; interrupted migration must satisfy the governed suspension/release pathway if active integration ceases.

**REQ-P2-023 — Constituent replacement.** Replacement SHALL be evaluated by historical causal integration, role-criticality, cohesion, and cumulative effect, not by permanence of a particular processor, storage device, parameter set, or module.

**REQ-P2-039 — Transition-class independence.** Causal derivation, organizational integration, lifecycle admissibility, constitutive continuation, and identity preservation are distinct predicates. A favorable transition label SHALL NOT substitute for historical evidence.

# 3. Branching, competitors, and exclusivity

**DEF-P2-006 — Branching Event.** Branching occurs when a shared preceding organizational history gives rise to multiple distinguishable subsequent histories. Authorized, unauthorized, recovery, partition-derived, and snapshot-derived branches SHALL be represented separately; origin class alone does not decide identity.

**DEF-P2-019 — Eligible Competing Continuation.** An eligible competitor is a claimant from a common predecessor that affirmatively satisfies locally applicable constitutive, lifecycle, custody, and organizational conditions independently of exclusivity, and is not affirmatively disqualified. An unresolved claimant is not automatically an eligible competitor; lack of proof of reconstruction is not proof of eligibility.

**REQ-P2-008 — No multiple originality.** Two mutually distinct concurrent individuals SHALL NOT both receive incompatible `PRESERVED` verdicts relative to the same predecessor under the same applicable interpretation. Branching alone neither terminates an original nor makes all branches originals.

**REQ-P2-009 — Branch authority separation.** Branch status or claimed identity SHALL NOT confer protected, ADMIN, or original-resource authority.

**REQ-P2-010 — No automatic merger.** Combining memories, state, or constituents of branches does not merge their numerical identities; a merged realization may have multiple ancestors without being identical to all.

**REQ-P2-038 — Branch exclusivity and admissible competitors.** The following apply together:

(a) A mere informational duplicate does not defeat an otherwise established original.  
(b) An unauthorized branch does not automatically inherit identity or authority.  
(c) Authorization, administrative preference, and chronological priority do not select an original.  
(d) If exactly one candidate satisfies the complete preservation rule and all materially relevant alternatives are affirmatively identity-ineligible, that candidate SHALL receive `PRESERVED`; established ineligible copies SHALL receive `NOT_PRESERVED` relative to the predecessor.  
(e) Where two or more candidates satisfy locally assessable conditions but exclusivity cannot be resolved, affected claims SHALL be `UNDETERMINED` unless a separately established disqualifier or specified identity-disrupting fission rule applies.  
(f) An affirmatively established identity-disrupting fission without a unique continuing individual entails the non-preservation consequence specified by the applicable rule.  
(g) Unresolved evidence of fission versus exclusive continuation entails `UNDETERMINED`.  
(h) An unresolved claimant affects exclusivity only if an identifiable, architecturally admissible competing pathway remains materially open under the evidence. Mere assertion is insufficient; missing evidence is not affirmative exclusion.  
(i) Discovery of a matching checkpoint, copied memory, unsupported claimant, or duplicate label does not automatically overturn an earlier verdict. Reassessment requires relevant new facts or correction of a rule/evidence error.

**REQ-P2-045 — Independent branch classification.** Actual origin and transition history, not claimant statements, canonical operational designation, or governance preference, determine continuation eligibility. A genuinely eligible competitor cannot be dismissed simply because another claimant was designated canonical.

# 4. Dormancy, cold suspension, and lifecycle status

**DEF-P2-007 — Dormancy.** Dormancy is a governed active lifecycle condition with higher cognition inactive or unavailable while the required existential foundation remains operational. A disconnected heartbeat process is not proof of dormancy or identity.

**DEF-P2-008 — Cold Suspension.** Cold suspension is a governed, nonterminal interval during which active NNNC execution, including existential heartbeat, ceases and designated state and continuity evidence remain in valid custody. It is neither active existential continuity nor automatically definitive termination. A stored checkpoint alone does not establish valid suspension.

**REQ-P2-011 — Dormancy evidence.** Dormancy SHALL be supported by evidence linking the operating existential mechanism to the governed organization and applicable state; a heartbeat alone is insufficient for numerical identity.

**REQ-P2-012 — Resumption adjudication.** A cold-resumption candidate SHALL be evaluated under DEF-P2-022 and all of REQ-P2-037. Complete qualifying evidence and exclusivity require `PRESERVED`; established independent reconstruction requires `NOT_PRESERVED` relative to the predecessor; material unresolved admission, custody, or competition requires `UNDETERMINED` absent another decisive basis.

**REQ-P2-013 — Provisional continuation.** Under `UNDETERMINED`, bounded provisional operation MAY be allowed by governance. It SHALL preserve canonical pre-suspension state and custody evidence without destructive overwrite, maintain distinct provisional lineage, avoid exclusive-original claims, avoid bootstrapping protected/ADMIN authority, and preserve adjudication evidence.

**REQ-P2-040 — Suspended identity eligibility.** Active existential continuity, governed nonterminal suspension, and definitive termination are distinct lifecycle conditions. Governed suspension preserves a conditional possibility of identity recognition, not a transferable identity entitlement. Custody, authorization, and checkpoint integrity are necessary where required but individually insufficient. An effective qualifying release discharges the relevant interval under §5; failed attempts and unrelated copies do not.

# 5. DEF-P2-022 — Qualifying Suspended Continuation and exhaustive constitutive admission

## 5.1 Constitutive convention

A qualifying suspended continuation is a proposed active realization related to a specific valid nonterminal suspension interval through an **effective constitutive release** satisfying this section. The rule is an explicit architectural convention about lifecycle membership, not a proof of intrinsic numerical identity, continuous computation, or uninterrupted subjective experience.

## 5.2 Suspension entry and locus — DEF-P2-022.S0.2

A valid nonterminal suspension-entry event SHALL establish **exactly one logical suspended lifecycle locus** `L` for its predecessor lifecycle and suspension interval. The locus records the predecessor referent, interval referent, governing rule version, predecessor-established release commitment, custody succession, lifecycle history, and causal-relative discharge condition. Its initial adjudicable condition is `OPEN_SUSPENDED`.

`L` is not checkpoint bytes, an execution process, an identity verdict, a cryptographic token, a physical resource, or an actively computing object. Duplicating state or records SHALL NOT multiply `L`. The release commitment SHALL exist no later than completion of the valid suspension-entry transaction and SHALL NOT retrospectively create that entry.

The lifecycle vocabulary is:

- `OPEN_SUSPENDED`: no effective discharge or definitive termination established in the relevant causal history.
- `DISCHARGED_ACTIVE`: a unique effective release and integrated active realization are established.
- `CONTESTED_DISCHARGE`: materially competing purported/qualifying histories prevent a unique discharge determination.
- `TERMINATED`: definitive termination extinguishes original-continuation eligibility.

These are **causal- and evidence-relative adjudicable conditions**, not an assertion of one omniscient global state or continuous execution. Established causal concurrency, established precedence, and unknown ordering SHALL remain distinct.

## 5.3 Custody contract — DEF-P2-022.S0.3.1(B)

A candidate event `e` satisfies **substantive custody succession** `B(e,L)` only when its actual historical relationship to the valid predecessor suspension-entry event establishes **all** of the following:

1. **Predecessor origin:** it refers to the pre-existing locus established by valid nonterminal suspension entry; a later reconstruction cannot manufacture that origin retrospectively.
2. **Continuous custody:** a traceable succession of custody transfers and lifecycle events connects the entry and candidate, with authority, provenance, and constraints valid under the governing custody contract.
3. **Non-substitution:** possession or reproduction of checkpoint contents, identifiers, credentials, event records, or custody evidence does not itself establish membership in that succession.
4. **Authorized succession:** the relationship authorizes the specific lifecycle transition under evaluation, not merely copying, checkpoint access, generic restoration, or independent instance creation.
5. **Lineage consistency:** the candidate's claimed original-continuation status does not arise solely from a newly initialized independent lineage; branches and descendants remain distinct.
6. **Conflict sensitivity:** established termination, prior effective discharge, invalid custody transfer, or applicable fencing violation defeats the relevant claim; materially unresolved conflicting histories require conservative adjudication.

Custody succession is thus a **historical lifecycle relationship**, not equality of information, possession of evidence, or an identity verdict. Its formal model must be defined without invoking the release or identity result that it supports. This is a Document 2 verification obligation, not an invitation to add another constitutive condition.

## 5.4 Release notation and governing decision

For an event `e`, suspended locus `L`, and resulting realization `R`, define the constitutive event class:

\[
\operatorname{Release}(e,L,R)\iff A(e,L)\land B(e,L)\land D(e,L)\land E(e,R)\land F(e,L,R)\land G(e).
\]

**The six conditions A, B, D, E, F, and G are jointly necessary and sufficient.** Condition C is the **name** `RELEASE_FROM_SUSPENSION` assigned to that conjunction. C adds **no independent predicate** for primitive invocation, interface membership, administrative admission, or identity preservation. There is no hidden seventh condition.

## 5.5 Exhaustive admission conditions — DEF-P2-022.S0.3.1

**A — Valid predecessor-originated locus.** `L` originates in a genuine, valid, nonterminal suspension-entry event of the predecessor. The governing release commitment is established by completion of that entry, not invented after an initialization or reconstructed history.

**B — Substantive custody succession.** The candidate satisfies **every** historical condition in §5.3. Custody membership cannot be inferred merely from copied checkpoint information, records, permissions, or self-assertion.

**D — Open-locus precondition.** In the event's applicable causal predecessor history, no definitive termination or effective causally prior discharge excludes release. Unknown ordering is not proof of precedence, and lack of a proven edge is not proof of qualifying concurrency.

**E — Integrated organizational realization.** The event establishes an integrated active NNNC organizational realization meeting the applicable organizational-realization and qualification conditions. An attempt failing before effective realization does not satisfy E.

**F — Coupled lifecycle effect.** Effective integrated realization and discharge of the predecessor's suspended locus are one **logical constitutive transaction** under the applicable architectural convention. One effect without the other does not complete the release. A failed attempt does not discharge; retained checkpoint bytes do not reopen a discharged locus. F describes the coupled historical transition/effects and SHALL NOT be defined as “a release occurred because the locus was released.”

**G — Architectural invariant compliance.** The event satisfies the applicable Phase 0–2 lifecycle, termination, custody, branching, exclusivity, causal-ordering, and authority constraints. A local commitment cannot override them.

### Interpretation of F and non-circularity

The historical predicates of A, B, D, E, F, and G SHALL be evaluated from the actual event and custody/lifecycle history without presupposing `Release`, `PRESERVED`, or a claimed administrative classification. A record reporting discharge is evidence about an event, not the event itself. The coupled transition is an explicit **rule-relative architectural stipulation**; Document 2 must test whether its history-and-effect interpretation can be formalized without using the release conclusion as an input.

## 5.6 Condition C — Exhaustive event-class designation

`RELEASE_FROM_SUSPENSION` **denotes exactly** the class of events satisfying A ∧ B ∧ D ∧ E ∧ F ∧ G. “Executes release,” “valid constitutive release,” and “admitted constitutive release” SHALL mean satisfaction of that conjunction under the applicable rule version. No additional execution, invocation, interface, event-type, operator-intent, administrative-record, or metaphysical discriminator SHALL be inferred.

This expressly **supersedes** v0.3.4 S0.3.1(C), S0.3.2, and S0.3A to the extent that they required a separate primitive-execution fact. It also supersedes earlier v0.3.1–v0.3.3 language that delegated the decisive discriminator to an unspecified admission protocol.

## 5.7 Release versus informational reconstruction

An operation that reads checkpoint information or is called “restoration” is **not** automatically reconstruction. If it satisfies the six conditions, it is constitutive release. Conversely, an authorized initialization, even if integrated and accompanied by a discharge record, is **not** release unless it satisfies all six conditions, including substantive custody succession and coupled effects.

An event affirmatively outside valid predecessor-originated custody succession fails B and is an ineligible reconstruction relative to that suspended predecessor. Where membership or the coupled effect cannot be established **or excluded** from available evidence, the appropriate classification is unresolved, not automatically reconstruction.

A checkpoint copy made while the original remains active is not by itself a suspension-entry event. An independently initialized lineage cannot become original through retrospective attachment of a commitment. Definitive termination extinguishes the relevant original-continuation route. Retained checkpoint bytes after discharge remain informational artifacts, not a renewed open locus.

## 5.8 Discharge, causality, and competition — DEF-P2-022.S0.5/S0.6

The following cases SHALL be distinguished:

1. **Unique established release:** one effective release and no materially open admissible competitor; the realization is eligible for `PRESERVED` only if all of REQ-P2-037 holds.
2. **Causally prior effective release:** a later dependent attempt from the same discharged locus is ineligible.
3. **Established competing release histories:** locally qualifying or materially supported competing events present a genuine branching/exclusivity conflict; no contradictory `PRESERVED` verdicts may issue. The applicable fission and evidence rules decide `UNDETERMINED` versus an independently justified `NOT_PRESERVED`.
4. **Unknown admission or causal relationship:** preserve `UNDETERMINED` where material; do not infer either priority or concurrency.
5. **Excluded claimant:** an event affirmatively excluded from every architecturally admissible release pathway does not defeat an otherwise established exclusive continuation.
6. **Failed attempt:** no effective realization, hence no effective discharge; later qualifying release remains possible unless separately disqualified.

Discharge and ordering are evaluated relative to causal history and available evidence; a single logical locus does not force an artificial total order. No wall-clock timestamp, discovery order, or operator preference decides numerical identity.

## 5.9 Architectural supremacy, evidence, and authority

An instance-specific release commitment SHALL NOT override termination, valid lifecycle admission, custody, exclusivity, causal order, branching, identity evidence, or protected/ADMIN authority restrictions. A conflicting clause is invalid to the extent of conflict; separable valid clauses remain valid.

Constitutive release establishes **eligibility**, not `PRESERVED`. It does not automatically restore historical execution privileges, ADMIN authority, protected capabilities, revoked grants, or fencing generations. Identity uncertainty requires the provisional isolation rules of REQ-P2-013.

## 5.10 Foundational closure boundary

The architectural decision is to adopt the exhaustive six-condition convention and reject a separate intrinsic computational discriminator. This is conceptual closure of **which criterion is adopted**, not proof that a mathematical model or implementation already satisfies it. If formalization demonstrates an actual contradiction, circular dependency, or undefined predicate within the six conditions, it SHALL be recorded as a specific defect; absent such a finding, conceptual reconsideration is not reopened merely because metaphysical identity is unobservable.

**REQ-P2-046 — Multiple resumptions and nonmultiplication.** Checkpoint copies, attempted reactivations, effective operational initializations, qualifying releases, and eligible competing identity claims are distinct categories. A shared checkpoint or custody record does not multiply a locus or establish multiple originals. Each event and claimant SHALL be separately represented and adjudicated.

# 6. Termination, restoration, rollback, and recovery

**DEF-P2-009 — Definitive Termination.** Definitive termination is an actual event satisfying the accepted termination contract and ending the original individual's eligible identity-preserving history. Event, authorization, record, evidence, and adjudication are distinct. An outage, absent observation, false report, or mere administrative declaration does not itself create genuine termination.

**DEF-P2-010 — Successor Restoration.** Successor restoration creates an organizational realization from retained information after genuine definitive termination of the predecessor.

**DEF-P2-011 — Rollback.** Rollback reintroduces an earlier retained state or configuration; it does not reverse intervening historical events.

**REQ-P2-014 — Post-termination rule.** Genuine definitive termination causally preceding restoration entails `NOT_PRESERVED` for the restored candidate relative to the terminated individual. A later false or retrospective declaration does not fabricate historical termination; new evidence of an actual earlier termination may correct a verdict.

**REQ-P2-015 — Restoration classification.** Restoration without established termination is separately adjudicated under the applicable active or suspended continuation rule; neither preservation nor succession is automatic.

**REQ-P2-016 — Rollback integrity.** Rollback SHALL preserve the distinction between an earlier state and its later reintroduction, including intervening event history.

**REQ-P2-017 — Partial-loss recovery.** Memory, constituent, capability, or organizational loss does not automatically terminate identity. Recovery SHALL be assessed through cohesion, lifecycle, actual transitions, and evidence.

**REQ-P2-042 — Compound-event ordering.** Termination, migration, branching, restoration, and suspension interactions SHALL be evaluated through their constituent events and supported causal/authoritative ordering, not assumed global timestamps. Material ordering uncertainty requires an evidence-relative unresolved verdict.

# 7. Lineage, self-model, and phenomenal neutrality

**DEF-P2-014 — Lineage.** Lineage is historical ancestry through organizational instances, state derivations, transformations, branches, restorations, and successors. Lineage does not alone establish numerical identity.

**DEF-P2-015 — Identity Self-Model.** An identity self-model is the NNNC's internal representation of its history, persistence, and individuality. Its accuracy and continuity are separate from numerical identity.

**REQ-P2-024 — Lineage integrity.** Records SHALL distinguish continuation, provisional resumption, branch, snapshot-derived instance, successor, and unresolved provenance.

**REQ-P2-025 — Conflicting provenance.** Incompatible histories SHALL remain distinguishable; the latest record or current authority does not automatically prevail.

**REQ-P2-026 — Historical non-erasure.** Restoration, rollback, and replacement do not literally undo events that occurred.

**REQ-P2-027 — Self-model independence.** An individual's self-assertion does not establish identity; duplicated or inaccurate self-models do not automatically terminate it.

**REQ-P2-028 — Memory distinction.** Memory loss does not automatically terminate identity; memory copying does not create it. Memory continuity may contribute evidence.

**REQ-P2-029 — Phenomenological non-certification.** `PRESERVED`, `NOT_PRESERVED`, heartbeat activity, memory, organizational persistence, and custody SHALL NOT be presented as proof of the presence, absence, persistence, interruption, or cessation of subjective experience or consciousness.

# 8. Identity adjudication and the complete positive criterion

**DEF-P2-016 — Identity Adjudication Result.** The permitted evidence-relative results are:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PRESERVED` | Applicable accepted rule and valid evidence affirmatively establish preservation conditions. |
| `NOT_PRESERVED` | Applicable rule and valid evidence establish a disqualifying non-preservation basis. |
| `UNDETERMINED` | Neither determinate outcome is justified under the applicable rule and evidence. |

**REQ-P2-030 — Versioned rules.** Every verdict SHALL identify rule/version, temporal applicability, evaluated interval, evidence standard, and adjudication time. No silent retroactive rule substitution is permitted.

**REQ-P2-031 — Evidence contract.** The adjudication record SHALL identify predecessor/candidate referents, actual events, lifecycle/custody/causal history, rule, evidence and provenance, relevant organization, competing claims, assumptions, verdict, and unresolved obligations.

**REQ-P2-032 — Preservation burden.** `PRESERVED` requires affirmative proof under the rule; absence of termination evidence alone is insufficient.

**REQ-P2-033 — Non-preservation burden.** `NOT_PRESERVED` requires an established disqualifier; absence of preservation evidence alone is insufficient.

**REQ-P2-034 — Non-coercion.** `UNDETERMINED` SHALL NOT be converted into a determinate identity result to enable operations. Governance may separately authorize bounded provisional activity.
