 0. Constitution, purpose and authority

 0.1 Purpose
 This specification establishes the foundational constitutional architecture of the Neutral Neural
 Network Core (NNNC).

 It defines the normative conditions under which an NNNC organizational realization may be
 constituted, governed, evaluated, and constitutionally transformed.

 It extends the accepted architectural commitments of:

    • Phase 0 — Ontological Boundary.

    • Phase 1 — Foundational Ontology.

    • Phase 2 — Identity & Continuity.

 It does not replace their existential, organizational, identity, custody, or protected-governance
 contracts.





 0.2 Constitutional authority

 REQ-P3-001 — Predecessor preservation
 Every constitutional rule introduced by Phase 3 SHALL preserve the controlling requirements of
 Phases 0–2 unless a separately authorized architectural revision explicitly changes those
 requirements.

 A Phase 3 rule SHALL NOT independently authorize such a revision.

 REQ-P3-002 — Constitutional non-duplication
 The Phase 3 constitution SHALL be interpreted as an elaboration of the versioned Constitutive
 Specification established by DEF-P1-019.

 It SHALL NOT create a second authoritative constitutive specification, existential authority, or
 numerical-identity criterion.

 REQ-P3-003 — Specification–realization separation
 The existence, validity, or completeness of a constitutional specification SHALL NOT establish
 that a corresponding NNNC organizational instance exists or conforms.

 0.3 Normative language
 SHALL indicates a binding requirement. SHALL NOT indicates a prohibition. MAY indicates
 permission subject to applicable constraints.

 Requirements apply within their stated scope and version. Their validity is not inferred merely
 from their inclusion in this draft.

 Citations carry their document prefix where an identifier is reused across documents (for
 example P1-D2:D2-INV-012 is provisional isolation; P2-D2:D2-INV-012 is negative burden). P1-D2
 verification obligations are cited as P1-D2:VO-nn to distinguish them from P2-VO-nn and P3-VO-
 nn.


 1. Constitutional ontology

 1.1 Fundamental categories

 DEF-P3-001 — Constitutional Framework
 The constitutional framework is the version-relative collection of constitutive rules, role
 definitions, competence assignments, consistency constraints, transformation requirements, and
 conformance obligations governing an NNNC organizational realization.

 It is part of the applicable Constitutive Specification, not an additional NNNC entity.

 DEF-P3-002 — Constitutional Rule
 A constitutional rule is a version-relative normative statement that constrains the admissibility,
 responsibility, relationship, evaluation, or transformation of specified organizational conditions.

 Every constitutional rule SHALL possess an identifiable scope and applicability condition.





 DEF-P3-003 — Constitutional Role
 A constitutional role is a specification-defined position of responsibility within the NNNC's
 organizational architecture.

 A role may be unfilled, filled by an admissible constituent, or subject to explicit cardinality
 constraints.

 A role's definition does not establish the existence of a corresponding constituent.

 DEF-P3-004 — Constitutional Competence
 Constitutional competence is the version-relative assignment of a defined responsibility or
 decision function to a constitutional role.

 Competence is distinct from technical capability, possession of resources, custody, trust, and
 operational authorization.

 DEF-P3-005 — Constitutional Obligation
 A constitutional obligation is a rule-defined requirement applicable to a specified role,
 organizational state, event, transition, or evaluation context.

 An obligation may be satisfied, violated, or unresolved under available evidence. Those
 evidentiary classifications SHALL remain distinct from the obligation's underlying truth
 conditions.

 DEF-P3-006 — Constitutional Validity
 Constitutional validity is the condition under which a constitutional rule or specification version
 is legitimately applicable within an authoritative governance history.

 Validity depends on applicable predecessor and adoption requirements, not on a rule's self-
 declaration.

 DEF-P3-007 — Constitutional Conformance
 Constitutional conformance is the relation between an organizational realization, an applicable
 specification version, and satisfaction of the requirements governing that realization.

 Evidence for conformance and certification of conformance are distinct from the underlying
 conformance relation.

 DEF-P3-016 — Exclusive Scope
 An exclusive scope is a scope within which at most one determination of a given kind may be
 authoritative at a time under the applicable version.

 Distinct scopes, and concurrent nonconflicting operations within one scope, do not by
 themselves constitute an exclusive-scope conflict.

 1.3 Ontological non-collapse

 REQ-P3-004 — Relation separation
 Constitutional role membership, competence, resource custody, operational control, trust, and
 legitimate authorization SHALL remain distinct.

 REQ-P3-005 — No competence escalation
 A constitutional competence assignment SHALL NOT independently grant protected execution,
 approval, certification, or administrative authority.

 REQ-P3-046 — No authorization-based competence
 Holding protected authorization for a transition SHALL NOT independently establish
 constitutional competence for the corresponding responsibility.

 Source: architect decision AD-04 (21 October 2026); no predecessor source. Together with REQ-

 Note (non-normative): this requirement adds no competence precondition to protected

 REQ-P3-006 — No nominal realization
 A component SHALL NOT be considered constitutionally realized solely because its name
 matches a specified role or domain.

 Actual realization requires the applicable organizational and conformance conditions.

 2. Constitutional validity and applicability

 2.1 Versioned constitutional states
 An NNNC constitutional history may contain multiple specification versions.

 However, the mere presence of multiple versions does not imply that all are simultaneously
 authoritative for the same decision.

 DEF-P3-008 — Applicable Constitutional Version
 The applicable constitutional version is the version determined for a particular scope, event, or
 evaluation by the independently valid predecessor-governed applicability rules.

 An applicable version SHALL NOT be chosen merely because it produces a preferred outcome.

 2.2 Validity requirements

 REQ-P3-007 — Independent applicability
 The applicability of a constitutional version SHALL be determined without allowing that version
 to establish its own adoption authority.

 REQ-P3-008 — Version-relative interpretation
 Constitutional requirements SHALL be interpreted under the version applicable to the relevant
 event or evaluation.




 For transitions, see REQ-P3-036.

 REQ-P3-009 — Unresolved applicability
 Where competing versions affect a material determination and no valid reconciliation rule
 establishes which governs, the constitutional applicability determination SHALL remain
 unresolved.

 Unresolved applicability SHALL NOT be converted into authorization by default.

 Only versions with valid adoption provenance (REQ-P3-035) count as competing versions under
 this requirement. A proposed, unadopted, or invalidly adopted version SHALL NOT render a
 determination unresolved.

 REQ-P3-010 — Historical non-retroactivity
 A later constitutional amendment SHALL NOT rewrite the historical validity conditions under
 which an earlier transition occurred.

 A later version MAY change only the prospective consequences of earlier events. The
 classification of an earlier transition under the version then applicable SHALL be retained. Any
 later-version assessment of that transition SHALL be recorded as a distinct, prospectively
 effective record alongside the retained classification; it SHALL NOT replace that classification or
 alter the transition's historical validity, and SHALL NOT fabricate prior authority.

 Phase 2's versioned verdict revision (P2-D2 P2-PF-08) is an analogy only; identity verdicts remain
 separate from constitutional validity.

 REQ-P3-035 — Genesis adoption
 The initial constitutional version (the genesis version) is applicable because it was adopted
 under the trusted governance bootstrap assumed by P1-D1 ASM-P1-001 and established under P0-
 D1 §5.4 (Trusted Bootstrap). This basis SHALL be recorded as an explicit external import; the
 genesis version SHALL NOT establish or validate its own adoption authority.

 This requirement does not identify the holder of the initial authority; P0-D1 §5.4 requires the
 implementation to identify how initial administrative authority is established. As required by
 ASM-P1-001, the bootstrap assumption SHALL be identified in verification results and SHALL NOT
 be represented as proof of an uncompromised root of trust. Ordinary NNNC cognitive
 components SHALL NOT replace the trusted authority root through their own modification
 operations (P0-D1 §5.4).

 Every later constitutional version SHALL be applicable only if it is connected to the genesis
 version by a finite, acyclic chain of ValidAmendment steps. Where no such chain is established,
 the version's applicability is invalid or unresolved, and never authorized by default.

 REQ-P3-036 — Commit-time governing version
 A transition, including a constitutional amendment, SHALL be judged under the governance
 state and constitutional version in force immediately before its commit (P1-D1 REQ-P1-033; P1-
 D2:D2-INV-007).





 An authorization given under an earlier version counts only if it is still valid under the version
 in force immediately before commit. Revocation before commit is one way of failing this check
 (P0-D1 §5.3).

    • If the check fails before commit, the transition SHALL be blocked.

    • If a transition committed although its authorization is known to have failed this check, the
      verdict SHALL be "committed without valid authorization", which is a nonconformance, not
      UNDETERMINED.

    • The verdict is UNDETERMINED only where the evidence or ordering cannot settle which
      version was in force, or the versions cannot be reconciled to one. UNDETERMINED SHALL
      NOT be treated as authorization by default (REQ-P3-009).

    • Once a transition has committed, no later version SHALL invalidate it or alter its retained
      classification (REQ-P3-010). A later-version assessment recorded alongside the retained
      classification under REQ-P3-010 is not a reclassification.

 2.3 Constitutional validity is not existential validity

 REQ-P3-011 — Existential independence
 A constitutionally valid specification SHALL NOT, by itself, establish that an NNNC exists,
 remains existentially active, or preserves numerical identity.

 Those determinations remain governed by their respective predecessor contracts.


 3. Constitutional competence and responsibility

 3.1 Competence allocation
 The constitution must make organizational responsibilities explicit without assuming that a
 single centralized controller performs them.

 DEF-P3-009 — Competence Domain
 A competence domain is a defined class of responsibilities, determinations, or organizational
 activities assigned to one or more constitutional roles.

 A competence domain is not necessarily an executable subsystem.

 DEF-P3-010 — Competence Boundary
 A competence boundary identifies the conditions under which a constitutional role may exercise
 a specified responsibility, including dependencies, exclusions, and required authorization.

 REQ-P3-012 — Explicit competence allocation
 Every constitutionally significant responsibility SHALL have a defined competence domain and
 an identifiable assignment rule.

 REQ-P3-038 — Competence assignment changes
 Creating, modifying, or removing a constitutional role definition (DefinesRole) or a competence
 assignment (AssignsCompetence) is a constitutional amendment and SHALL satisfy §5.





 A change of role occupancy (FillsRole) is not an amendment. Where the role carries any of the
 four authorities distinguished by P1-D1 REQ-P1-033 (propose, approve, execute, certify) or ADMIN
 CLASS content, the change SHALL be a protected transition governed by the Phase 0 trusted
 control plane (P0-D1 §5.1) and SHALL require an authenticated principal and valid delegation
 (P0-O06, open). Otherwise the change SHALL follow the applicable version's assignment rule
 (REQ-P3-012).

 A constituent SHALL NOT assign itself to a role through its own operation (P0-D1 §5.2; P0-D1
 OB-07).

 Note (non-normative): this requirement does not narrow Phase 0 governance of membership
 changes or protected state mutation (P0-D1 §5.1; REQ-P3-001).

 REQ-P3-013 — Unassigned responsibilities
 Where the applicable specification provides no assignment rule for a mandatory responsibility,
 that SHALL be treated as a specification incompleteness.

 Where the specification assigns a mandatory responsibility to a role but the role is unfilled in an
 organizational instance, that SHALL be treated as an instance nonconformance.

 It SHALL NOT automatically be assigned to the nearest available component.

 REQ-P3-014 — Overlapping competence
 Multiple roles MAY participate in the same organizational process where their responsibilities
 are explicitly distinguishable or their shared competence is governed by a defined coordination
 contract.

 Overlapping competence SHALL NOT automatically establish conflicting authority.

 REQ-P3-015 — Competence conflict
 Where incompatible competence assignments apply to the same exclusive decision, the conflict
 SHALL be resolved by an independently valid rule or remain unresolved.

 A component SHALL NOT settle such a conflict merely by executing its preferred decision first.

 3.2 Architectural non-substitution

 REQ-P3-016 — Mandatory architectural preservation
 The constitutional framework SHALL preserve the adopted mandatory architectural floor,
 including:

    • The five FFNN flows: Forward, Reverse, Intermediate, Subconscious, and Meta.

    • The six distinct Systemic Algorithms: Intelligence, Reasoning, Critical Thinking, Symmetry,
      Evolution, and Life.

    • The remaining mandatory architectural domains inherited from Phase 1.

 REQ-P3-017 — Functional non-substitution
 A general-purpose neural model, reinforcement-learning algorithm, LLM, external tool, or
 ordinary software controller SHALL NOT satisfy a mandatory architectural domain merely
 through nominal assignment or superficial functional similarity.





 Such mechanisms MAY serve as genuine subcomponents where permitted by the eventual
 authoritative realization contracts.

 REQ-P3-018 — Internal causal responsibility
 Where a function is claimed as internally realized, the applicable conformance contract SHALL
 require evidence of a meaningful internal causal contribution, judged by the discriminating
 criterion defined in the applicable domain realization contract.

 Phase 3 does not define that criterion; detailed realization requirements remain assigned to the
 designated architectural domains (P1-D1).

 A pass-through interface, mandatory invocation, or output-formatting layer is insufficient.

 These requirements preserve the existing Phase 1 architectural commitments. They do not
 prematurely define how the FFNN or Systemic Algorithms must be implemented.


 4. Constitutional consistency and conflicts

 4.1 Consistency model

 DEF-P3-011 — Constitutional Conflict
 A constitutional conflict occurs when simultaneously applicable requirements impose
 incompatible obligations, prohibitions, competence assignments, or admissibility conditions
 within the same relevant scope.

 A difference in objectives or component outputs is not necessarily a constitutional conflict.

 DEF-P3-012 — Constitutional Resolution Rule
 A constitutional resolution rule is an independently applicable rule specifying how a defined
 class of constitutional conflicts is to be interpreted or resolved.

 Resolution rules do not create protected authorization merely by selecting a preferred outcome.

 DEF-P3-020 — Constitutional Consistency
 The absence of incompatible simultaneously applicable constitutional requirements, subject to
 explicitly defined precedence, scope, and conflict-resolution relationships.

 Unresolved conflicts must remain distinguishable from demonstrated consistency.

 REQ-P3-019 — Conflict identification
 The constitutional framework SHALL distinguish genuine incompatibility from permissible
 functional diversity, concurrent activity, and incomplete evidence.

 REQ-P3-020 — Non-arbitrary precedence
 Where precedence among constitutional requirements is necessary, its applicability SHALL be
 established by an authorized version-relative rule.

 No component SHALL acquire precedence merely by asserting higher importance.





 Precedence between Phase 3 and its predecessors is governed by REQ-P3-037. Precedence among
 requirements in force under the same constitutional version is governed by REQ-P3-045. A
 conflict that neither resolves remains unresolved under REQ-P3-021.

 REQ-P3-037 — Predecessor precedence
 Where a Phase 3 rule conflicts with a requirement of an accepted predecessor document of
 Phases 0–2, the predecessor requirement SHALL prevail (REQ-P3-001; for Phase 0, P0-D1 §0.2).

 Precedence under this requirement applies only among accepted documents. A proposed or
 unadopted document confers no precedence.

 P1-D1 v2.0 and P2-D1 v1.0 are accepted (architect decision AD-02), as are P1-D2 v0.2.1 (AD-01)
 and P2-D2 v1.0 (approved, CONDITIONAL PASS); the Phase 0 baseline is conditionally accepted
 (P0-D1 v1.3 control block: "Architectural baseline: Conditionally accepted, 18 October 2026"; P0-
 D2 v1.3.1 Normative Status). For this requirement, Phase 0's conditional acceptance counts as
 accepted: P0-D2 v1.3.1 treats it as architectural acceptance ("The specification is sufficiently
 defined to serve as a controlled foundation for subsequent work"), which is distinct from
 implementation or deployment conformance, and P1-D1 v2.0 names it as the "Accepted Phase 0
 architectural baseline". Its open conditions, including P0-O01..P0-O08, remain open (§7.2).

 As a general rule, pending an acceptance record, a predecessor that an accepted document
 names as its governing or authoritative basis SHALL be treated as prevailing. Uncertainty about
 a predecessor's acceptance SHALL NOT give Phase 3 precedence.

 Within a phase, Document 1 governs Document 2 (as recorded in P1-D2 and P2-D2 document
 control, which name their Document 1 as authoritative or governing basis).

 Phase 3 SHALL NOT resolve a conflict between predecessor requirements. Such a conflict SHALL
 be recorded as a numbered erratum (P3-ERR-nn) and escalated to the architect; the affected
 determination remains unresolved (REQ-P3-009) until resolved.

 REQ-P3-045 — Precedence within a version
 Where requirements in force under the same constitutional version conflict and REQ-P3-037
 does not resolve the conflict, precedence SHALL be determined by the following criteria, applied
 in strict lexical order:

    1. A protected-governance requirement prevails over an operational requirement.

    2. Between requirements of the same class (both protected-governance or both operational), a
       prohibition prevails over a permission.

    3. A specific requirement prevails over a general one.

 A later criterion applies only where no earlier criterion distinguishes between the conflicting
 requirements. A criterion does not distinguish between two requirements where both or neither
 fall in its favoured class. A specific requirement SHALL NEVER override a requirement that
 criterion 1 or criterion 2 favours over it.

 Note (non-normative): under criterion 1, a protected-governance requirement, whether a duty
 (SHALL) or a permission (MAY), prevails over a conflicting operational requirement, including
 an operational prohibition. A protected-governance permission therefore overrides operational
 limits, including operational safety limits; this is the cost accepted in AD-07. Prohibitions of
 Phases 0–2 still prevail under REQ-P3-037, which is applied first.





 For this requirement:

    • A protected-governance requirement is a requirement that governs a protected transition or
      operation under the Phase 0 trusted control plane (P0-D1 §5.1), ADMIN CLASS content (P0-D1
      OB-30), or one of the four authorities distinguished by P1-D1 REQ-P1-033 (propose, approve,
      execute, certify). Every other requirement is an operational requirement.

    • A requirement is more specific than another when the scope σ within which it applies, as in
      Applicable(r,σ,h,t) (§1.2), is a strict subset of the scope within which the other applies. Where

      Note (non-normative): this σ is a scope of application; it is not P1-D2's σ∈Σ, which denotes a
      versioned constitutive specification.

    • A requirement's own stated exceptions and limits are part of that requirement, not a
      conflicting permission.

 5. Constitutional transformation

 5.1 Amendment ontology

 DEF-P3-013 — Constitutional Amendment
 A constitutional amendment is a governed transition that changes constitutionally significant
 content of the applicable Constitutive Specification.

 It may affect roles, competence assignments, relationships, constraints, interfaces, or
 permissible organizational transformations.

 DEF-P3-014 — Constitutional Amendment Provenance
 Amendment provenance is the historical record of the proposal, authorization, commitment,
 and resulting specification version, subject to the applicable predecessor evidence and
 governance contracts.



 DEF-P3-017 — Ordinary Constitutional Amendment
 An ordinary constitutional amendment is a constitutional amendment within Phase 3
 constitutional-transformation competence that does not change predecessor requirements or
 entrenched content (REQ-P3-041).

 DEF-P3-018 — Architectural Revision
 An architectural revision is a change to predecessor requirements or to entrenched content. It is
 the separately authorized architectural revision referred to in REQ-P3-001, corresponding to
 Phase 0's "governed architectural revision" (P0-D1 §0.2) and Phase 1's "explicitly authorized
 architectural amendment" (P1-D1 REQ-P1-003).

 Architectural revision is outside Phase 3 competence. Phase 3 neither defines nor authorizes its
 process.

 DEF-P3-019 — Developmental Envelope
 A developmental envelope is the bounded class of changes to constitutionally significant
 organizational arrangements that a validly adopted version pre-authorizes to occur through
 developmental processes without a separate amendment.

 An envelope is defined only by a validly adopted version. It cannot be defined or extended by
 the developmental process it bounds, and it excludes ADMIN CLASS content (P0-D1 OB-30).

 5.2 Amendment requirements

 REQ-P3-023 — Prior authorization
 A constitutional amendment SHALL satisfy the applicable protected-transition authorization
 requirements under the valid pre-amendment governance state, determined as in REQ-P3-036
 (P1-D1 REQ-P1-033; P1-D2:D2-INV-007).

 The authority to propose, approve, execute, and certify an amendment SHALL be distinguished
 (REQ-P1-033). Approval authority SHALL NOT by itself confer certification authority for an
 amendment, nor the reverse (P1-D1 §4.4, "Separation of authority domains": "Neither authority
 automatically grants the other").

 REQ-P3-024 — Version lineage
 Every committed constitutional amendment SHALL preserve an identifiable relationship
 between its predecessor and successor specification versions.

 This relationship is not, by itself, evidence of numerical identity preservation.

 REQ-P3-025 — Amendment admissibility
 A proposed amendment SHALL be evaluated against the controlling architectural constraints
 and the applicable amendment rules.

 An inadmissible amendment SHALL NOT become valid merely because its resulting
 configuration can execute.

 REQ-P3-026 — Historical preservation
 Constitutional amendments SHALL NOT erase or retroactively legitimate historical violations,
 invalid custody acts, unauthorized transitions, or identity discontinuities.





 REQ-P3-027 — Controlled constitutional evolution
 Developmental changes MAY alter constitutionally significant organizational arrangements only
 within a developmental envelope pre-authorized in the applicable version (DEF-P3-019).

 Developmental plasticity SHALL NOT constitute unrestricted constitutional amendment
 authority.

 Ordinary cognitive, learning, developmental, and self-modification mechanisms SHALL NOT
 alter ADMIN CLASS implementation code, protected configuration, role contracts, or
 modification policies (P0-D1 OB-30). ADMIN CLASS protection is enforced by a trusted
 mechanism outside their ordinary modification authority (P0-D1 OB-39).

 REQ-P3-043 — Developmental change versus amendment
 A change SHALL be classified by its effect on constitutionally significant content, not by the
 mechanism producing it.

 A change is a constitutional amendment, subject to §5, if it changes constitutionally significant
 content outside the applicable developmental envelope, or if the cumulative effect of all changes
 since the last validly adopted version falls outside that envelope, even where each individual
 change was within it.

 Local admissibility of each step does not establish cumulative admissibility (P1-D2:D2-IND-014).
 Where the envelope or the cumulative effect cannot be evaluated, the change SHALL NOT be
 treated as a permitted developmental change.

 REQ-P3-028 — Concurrent amendments
 Where multiple constitutional amendments are proposed or committed concurrently, their
 compatibility and resulting applicability SHALL be determined by explicit governance and
 reconciliation contracts.

 A simple last-writer-wins rule SHALL NOT independently establish constitutional legitimacy.

 5.3 Amendment classes, entrenchment and reversion

 REQ-P3-040 — Amending the amendment rules
 An amendment that changes the amendment rules SHALL be evaluated entirely under the
 amendment rules in force immediately before it commits. Its new rules SHALL apply only to
 later amendments and SHALL NOT be applied to evaluate the amendment that introduces them.

 No amendment SHALL change, in the same step, the rule that authorizes it (P0-D1 §5.2).

 REQ-P3-041 — Entrenchment
 The following content is entrenched and SHALL NOT be changed by an ordinary constitutional
 amendment: REQ-P3-001 to REQ-P3-006, REQ-P3-010, REQ-P3-016, REQ-P3-023, REQ-P3-026, REQ-
 P3-033, REQ-P3-035, REQ-P3-036, REQ-P3-040, REQ-P3-041, REQ-P3-042, REQ-P3-045, REQ-P3-046,
 DEF-P3-017, DEF-P3-018, and the mandatory domain floor D_floor (P1-D1).

 A change to entrenched content is an architectural revision (DEF-P3-018). Removing an item
 from, or narrowing, the entrenched list in this requirement is itself a change to entrenched
 content. Phase 3 rules SHALL NOT themselves authorize an architectural revision (REQ-P3-001).





 The entrenchment of REQ-P3-035, REQ-P3-036, REQ-P3-040 and REQ-P3-042 rests on P0-D1 §5.2
 (no self-granted authority through the policy update being evaluated), §5.3, §5.4 (the trusted
 authority root cannot be replaced through ordinary modification) and §5.5, and on P1-D1 REQ-
 P1-033.


 REQ-P3-042 — Reversion is amendment
 Re-adopting the content of an earlier constitutional version is a constitutional amendment,
 never a reset. It SHALL satisfy REQ-P3-023 to REQ-P3-026 and REQ-P3-036.

 The re-adopted content becomes a new successor version in the lineage (REQ-P3-024); it SHALL
 NOT erase the intervening versions or their history (REQ-P3-026).

 A reversion SHALL also satisfy P0-D1 §5.5: unauthorized rollback to an earlier vulnerable policy
 is prohibited.


 6. Constitutional interfaces and conformance

 6.1 Interfaces

 DEF-P3-015 — Constitutional Interface Contract
 A constitutional interface contract specifies the obligations and constraints governing
 interactions between constitutionally significant roles, domains, or processes.

 It may identify required information, admissible dependencies, responsibility boundaries, and
 verification obligations.

 An interface contract is not evidence that its endpoints are implemented.

 REQ-P3-029 — Cross-domain responsibility
 Constitutionally significant interactions SHALL identify their responsible domains and
 applicable constraints.

 REQ-P3-030 — No implementation by declaration
 Declaring an FFNN flow, Systemic Algorithm, or other mandatory domain in a constitutional
 interface SHALL NOT establish its realization.

 REQ-P3-031 — Non-circular conformance evidence
 A conformance claim SHALL distinguish the requirement being evaluated, the observed
 evidence, and the authority or procedure evaluating that evidence.

 The candidate's own assertion SHALL NOT suffice for independent certification.

 REQ-P3-044 — Evaluator independence
 Independent evaluation for certification SHALL build on P1-D1 REQ-P1-030, which requires
 independent assessment and prevents a candidate's ordinary self-modification mechanisms
 from approving their own conformance profile.

 An evaluator is independent of a candidate only if it is not the candidate, is not controlled by the
 candidate or by the role that assigned the candidate's competence, and its verdict does not rest
 solely on evidence produced by the candidate.




 A conformance or certification claim SHALL NOT receive PASS without independent evaluation.
 Missing independent evaluation or missing evidence yields UNDETERMINED or
 NOT_YET_CERTIFIABLE (P1-D2:D2-REQ-003; P1-D2 §8 evaluation outcomes), never PASS.

 REQ-P3-032 — Honest qualification
 Where a mandatory realization contract remains incomplete or unverified, the corresponding
 complete-conformance claim SHALL remain uncertified.

 Partial implementation, simulation, experimental demonstration, and incomplete engineering
 qualification SHALL remain distinguishable.

 6.2 Phase 2 authority isolation

 REQ-P3-033 — Identity–authority separation
 An identity-preservation verdict SHALL NOT independently grant protected or administrative
 authority.

 An unresolved identity determination SHALL NOT bootstrap predecessor authority.

 REQ-P3-039 — Provisional operation competence
 The responsibility to permit bounded provisional operation under UNDETERMINED identity (P2-
 D1 REQ-P2-013) SHALL be assigned to an explicit competence domain defined in the applicable
 version.

 Each exercise of that competence SHALL satisfy ConstitutionallyAuthorized (which requires

 The competence domain itself grants no protected authority (REQ-P3-005).

 Where no such assignment exists, provisional operation SHALL NOT be treated as permitted

 Their actual correctness SHALL remain subject to independent contract verification.
