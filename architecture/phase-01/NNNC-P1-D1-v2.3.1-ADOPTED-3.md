0. Constitution, authority and scope

0.1 Purpose

This document defines the foundational ontological categories, constitutive relationships, architectural qualification conditions, temporal distinctions, and mathematical-formalization obligations of the Neutral Neural Network Core.

It establishes the conditions under which an NNNC may be described as a computational entity, evaluated as an architectural candidate, tested as a reference kernel and ultimately qualified as a conforming implementation.

It does not establish that an NNNC is conscious, biologically alive, phenomenally aware or morally significant.

0.2 Normative precedence

Phase 1 SHALL inherit the accepted Phase 0 architectural baseline.

Where Phase 1 introduces additional precision, it SHALL NOT silently override Phase 0's accepted commitments concerning:

• NNNC–NES separation.
• NNNC ownership of the existential heartbeat.
• Separation of heartbeat and recovery.
• ADMIN CLASS protection.
• Authorized suspension and shutdown.
• Lifecycle, functional-mode and component-health distinctions.
• Restoration-after-termination successor convention.
• Protected state and governance authority.
• Outstanding implementation and verification obligations.

A contradiction SHALL trigger a governed cross-phase review.

0.3 Statement classes

All normative statements SHALL be classified as one of the following:

Definitions do not automatically establish implementation conformance. Assumptions are not verified facts. Hypotheses are not accepted requirements unless separately adopted.

0.4 Architectural completeness and epistemic restraint

A specification may establish a binding architectural commitment before establishing every condition required to demonstrate its realization.

The existence of such a commitment SHALL NOT be interpreted as permission to replace its eventual authoritative requirements with weaker, locally invented criteria.

A requirement whose authoritative realization contract remains incomplete SHALL be classified as unresolved for complete conformance certification, even where preliminary implementations or simulations exist.

This restriction concerns certification and evidential sufficiency. It does not establish that every uncertified implementation is nonconforming in fact.

1. Foundational ontological construction

1.1 Construction order

The ontology SHALL be constructed without presupposing the identity of the entity being defined.

The logical construction order is:

1. Distinguishable referents, states and occurrences.
2. Primitive relations, governance rules and interfaces.
3. Constitutive specification and candidate organization.
4. Realized organizational instance.
5. Qualification, conformance and evidence assessment.

Identity and continuity are evaluated relative to this organization and its governed history. They are not assumed as unexplained prerequisites.

1.2 Distinct specification and instance categories

DEF-P1-019 — Constitutive Specification

A versioned, authoritative declarative model specifying the required constituent roles, relations, state classes, interfaces, governance contracts and permissible organizational transformations.

DEF-P1-020 — Organizational Instance

A temporally situated organization realized through computational resources and processes, whose actual structure and transitions may be evaluated against a constitutive specification.

A constitutive specification is not an organizational instance.

A manifest describing an instance is not evidence that the instance exists or conforms.

1.3 Organizational boundary

A boundary SHALL be understood as a rule-governed partition over distinguishable states, processes, interfaces and authority relationships.

Constitutive membership, resource custody, execution capability, control, trust and legitimate authority SHALL remain distinct relations.

A resource may be physically necessary to an NNNC without being a constituent of the NNNC.

2. Architectural qualification and conformance

2.1 Qualification classes

DEF-P1-021 — Level E: Computational Entity

A bounded, state-bearing computational organization with defined transition rules and distinguishable operational and referential properties.

Level E does not imply NNNC conformance.

DEF-P1-022 — Level C: NNNC Architectural Candidate

A Level E entity associated with an explicit NNNC conformance manifest identifying implemented, simulated, absent and deferred architectural requirements.

A Level C claim is an architectural-development classification, not evidence of complete conformance.

DEF-P1-023 — Level R: Foundational Reference Kernel

An implemented testbed that exercises identified foundational NNNC contracts without claiming complete NNNC realization.

Reference kernels MAY simulate mechanisms for experimental purposes, provided simulation is explicitly disclosed.

DEF-P1-024 — Level N: NNNC-Conformant Instance

An organizational instance satisfying the non-deferrable NNNC architectural floor and a valid, independently evaluated complete conformance profile.

DEF-P1-025 — Level K: Demonstrated Capability Classification

A capability-specific classification based on an identified evaluation contract and supporting operational evidence.

Level K is independent of architectural qualification and does not establish consciousness or moral status.

2.2 Profile validity

REQ-P1-030 — Independent Conformance-Profile Validity

Every Level N conformance profile SHALL:

• Reference an authoritative NNNC architectural version.
• Identify its own immutable version and integrity identifier.
• Include all mandatory non-deferrable requirements.
• Specify permissible implementation variations.
• Identify evaluation procedures and evidence thresholds.
• Identify profile authorship and approval authority.
• Prevent the candidate's ordinary self-modification mechanisms from approving their own conformance profile.
• Require independent assessment of profile validity before instance conformance is evaluated.

A self-declared profile SHALL NOT acquire normative authority merely because an implementation satisfies it.

2.3 Irreducible architectural floor

REQ-P1-031 — Non-Deferrable NNNC Commitments

A Level N profile SHALL require realized, non-simulated implementations of the following architectural domains:

The detailed realization requirements for the FFNN and six Systemic Algorithms remain assigned to their designated architectural domains.

A profile SHALL NOT replace an essential internal cognitive mechanism entirely with an external service while claiming that the mechanism is internally realized.

A profile that defers, stubs or simulates a mandatory domain SHALL NOT grant Level N.

Clarification introduced in prior: The table establishes indispensable architectural domains, not complete or sufficient implementation definitions for those domains.

In particular, the words FFNN, Intelligence, Reasoning, Critical Thinking, Symmetry, Evolution and Life SHALL NOT function as self-validating component labels.

The complete realization requirements of those mechanisms SHALL be determined by their authoritative specifications, not by examples invented for the purpose of conformance testing.

2.4 Conformance and claim discipline

The conformance level of a candidate is relative to a versioned architectural profile and an accepted architectural floor. Level N requires realization and evidence of all non-deferrable NNNC domains, not merely a profile's declarations. A profile cannot delete, stub or substitute a mandatory domain. Qualification, observed capability, numerical identity, and operational authorization remain separate judgments.

2.5 Architectural dependency versus realized satisfaction

DEF-P1-030 — Architectural Dependency

A binding obligation that a specified mechanism, relationship or capability form part of the intended architecture, independently of whether its complete realization criteria have already been formalized.

DEF-P1-031 — Verified Architectural Satisfaction

A conformance judgment supported by evidence that an identified realized mechanism satisfies the applicable authoritative specification and integration requirements.

Architectural dependency and verified architectural satisfaction are distinct.

A commitment may be binding while its realization remains unverified.

The absence of a completed realization specification SHALL NOT cause the commitment to disappear, become optional or be considered automatically satisfied.

2.6 Non-substitution of unspecified architectural commitments

REQ-P1-044 — Non-Substitution of Unspecified Architectural Commitments

An architectural component whose authoritative normative specification has not yet been completed SHALL NOT be considered satisfied through naming correspondence, superficial functional similarity, generic implementation patterns or locally invented substitute criteria.

References to the FFNN and the six Systemic Algorithms establish binding architectural dependencies, not permission to instantiate arbitrary simplified equivalents.

Until the relevant component specification, integration contract and conformance criteria have been accepted, the component's complete realization SHALL remain unverified and not yet certifiable.

An implementation MAY be classified as an experimental prototype, reference kernel or architectural candidate. It SHALL NOT obtain complete Level N conformance by exploiting the absence of a completed component specification.

Subsequent accepted specifications SHALL determine the requirements for component realization and integration. They SHALL NOT be presumed satisfied retroactively without appropriate evaluation.

2.6.1 Scope of the prohibition

REQ-P1-044 prohibits unsupported substitution claims.

It does not prohibit:

• Research prototypes.
• Simplified experimental models.
• Component-level simulations.
• Conventional algorithms used as legitimate subcomponents.
• Alternative implementations that satisfy the eventual authoritative contract.
• Comparative evaluation of different implementation strategies.

Such implementations SHALL be described according to what they actually realize and demonstrate.

They SHALL NOT be represented as complete implementations of a larger NNNC architectural component without satisfying that component's accepted realization contract.

2.6.2 Architectural composition

A conventional algorithm MAY contribute to the realization of a Systemic Algorithm.

A conventional neural-network mechanism MAY contribute to the realization of an FFNN flow.

Neither fact establishes that the complete Systemic Algorithm or FFNN flow has been realized.

Similarly, satisfying individual component contracts does not automatically establish satisfaction of their integration contract.

2.6.3 Non-retroactive certification

When an authoritative specification is completed or revised, earlier prototypes SHALL be evaluated against the applicable accepted requirements before receiving complete conformance certification.

Prior implementation dates, historical naming conventions or earlier provisional assessments SHALL NOT substitute for that evaluation.

2.6.4 Conformance classification during architectural development

Where a mandatory architectural contract remains incomplete:

• Its dependency status SHALL remain binding.
• Its realization status MAY be recorded as partially implemented, simulated, experimentally demonstrated or otherwise supported by evidence.
• Its complete satisfaction status SHALL remain uncertified.
• Complete Level N certification SHALL remain unavailable.

This restriction SHALL NOT be interpreted as a finding that every implementation awaiting certification is definitively nonconforming.

───

### Fixed architectural floor and honest qualification

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-031, REQ-P1-044, REQ-P1-059, REQ-P1-067 and REQ-P1-072.** Level N requires the entire adopted mandatory domain floor, including all five distinct FFNN flows (Forward, Reverse, Intermediate, Subconscious, Meta) and each of the six distinct Systemic Algorithms (Intelligence, Reasoning, Critical Thinking, Symmetry, Evolution, Life). Their eventual authoritative contracts must preserve upstream commitments and establish discriminating realization and integration conditions. A profile cannot shrink the floor. An ordinary algorithm may serve as a genuine subcomponent, but superficial renaming, simulation, wrappers, or omission cannot satisfy a mandatory domain. Level C and Level R are legitimate incomplete engineering statuses, not claims of full NNNC realization. Level K is capability-specific and does not by itself establish a Systemic Algorithm.

3. Organizational and cognitive unity

3.1 Distinct unity classes

DEF-P1-026 — Administrative Unity

An organization whose constituents are managed or governed through a common administrative framework.

DEF-P1-027 — Functional Unity

An organization whose constituents cooperate to perform identifiable functions.

DEF-P1-028 — Cognitive Unity

An organization in which internal cognitive and regulatory mechanisms participate in coordinated, causally consequential processing, with governed state relationships influencing subsequent internal activity and environmental interaction.

Administrative or functional unity SHALL NOT alone establish cognitive unity.

3.2 Integration evidence

REQ-P1-032 — NNNC-Specific Causal Integration

A Level N claim SHALL provide evidence that essential internal mechanisms participate in the claimed cognitive organization.

The evaluation contract SHALL include intervention tests addressing, where applicable:

• Cross-component state dependence.
• Effects of internal regulatory state on activity selection.
• Effects of memory state on subsequent processing.
• Consequences of removing or disabling role-critical constituents.
• Conflict resolution among competing internal regulatory processes.
• Whether claimed internal capabilities actually depend on external tools.

Successful message exchange or shared database access alone SHALL NOT establish cognitive unity.

Integration evidence SHALL be evaluated against the actual accepted component contracts. Demonstrating interaction among superficially named components SHALL NOT substitute for demonstrating the required interactions among the intended architectural mechanisms.

3.3 Role criticality

Every constituent role SHALL be classified as:

• Constitutively essential.
• Conditionally essential.
• Replaceable under an authorized transformation.
• Optional.

Role criticality SHALL be evaluated relative to a specified architectural version and operational mode.

───

### Causal unity and attribution

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-032, REQ-P1-047, REQ-P1-062 and REQ-P1-075.** Claimed internal capabilities require an internal, discriminating causal contribution to the decisive cognitive operation. A mere mandatory signature, formatting wrapper or pass-through gate does not establish that contribution. External services may assist; they do not become internal NNNC cognitive mechanisms by being called through an internal interface. Constituent-level and pairwise evidence alone cannot establish global organizational unity. Later evaluation contracts must include meaningful causal interventions and system-level failure cases; candidate self-declaration alone cannot waive an essential intervention.

4. Authority, governance and protected transitions

4.1 Independent authority relationships

The ontology SHALL distinguish:

ConstituentOf, Controls, HasCustody, Trusts, DependsOn, AuthorizedFor.

None of these relations SHALL be inferred automatically from another.

In particular:

[
Controls(x,y)\not\Rightarrow AuthorizedFor(x,a,y).
]

4.2 Pre-transition authorization

REQ-P1-033 — Authorization Non-Retroactivity

A protected transition SHALL be authorized under the valid authoritative governance state preceding its commitment.

A transition SHALL NOT establish the authority required to authorize itself through its own resulting state.

The contract SHALL distinguish authority to propose, approve, execute and certify a change.

Any permitted delegation or escalation SHALL derive from an independently valid prior authority chain.

4.3 Bootstrap assumptions

ASM-P1-001 — Trusted Governance Bootstrap

The reference model assumes a valid initial governance authority and enforcement configuration established under the accepted Phase 0 bootstrap contract.

This assumption SHALL be explicitly identified in verification results.

It SHALL NOT be represented as proof that an implementation possesses an uncompromised root of trust.

4.4 Concurrent authoritative changes

REQ-P1-035 — Authoritative Transition Consistency

Changes to constitutive membership, protected state, existential authority or governance permissions SHALL be governed by an explicit ordering and conflict-resolution contract.

Concurrent operations SHALL NOT both commit as authoritative when their combined effects violate an accepted safety invariant.

The contract SHALL define stale-authority rejection, revocation precedence, fencing epochs and indeterminate outcomes where appropriate.

No universal physical clock is required.

───

### Separation of authority domains

Operational governance authorizes protected runtime changes under Phase 0. External evaluation and certification authorize claims about conformance. Neither authority automatically grants the other. Their actual human/institutional independence is a declared external-assurance precondition, not an NNNC ontological property. No candidate-controlled mechanism may conclusively certify its own Level N conformance.

5. Specification, realization and evidence

5.1 Four-layer distinction

1. Normative specification

Defines what the architecture requires.

2. Formal model

Defines the mathematical abstraction and properties evaluated.

3. Realized implementation

Contains the mechanisms that actually execute and enforce the contract.

4. Verification evidence

Supports bounded claims about the evaluated model or implementation.

An implication between these layers SHALL NOT be assumed without an explicit validation relationship.

5.2 Evidence binding

REQ-P1-034 — Specification–Realization–Evidence Binding

Every Level N conformance claim SHALL identify:

• Applicable requirement and specification version.
• Actual implementation artifact and configuration.
• Constituent or mechanism responsible for satisfaction.
• Evaluation environment.
• Procedure and evaluation period.
• Observed results.
• Evidence provenance and integrity.
• Relevant assumptions, limitations and known faults.
• Independent evaluator or authorized verification process.

A manifest SHALL NOT serve as its own proof of implementation.

Evidence from one implementation version SHALL NOT automatically transfer to another.

For an FFNN or Systemic Algorithm realization claim, the applicable requirement SHALL refer to its authoritative component specification and integration contract, rather than to an independently invented simplified substitute.

5.3 Custodial integrity

REQ-P1-038 — Authoritative State Custody and Tamper-Evidence

Authoritative governance, membership, lineage and protected-state records SHALL be subject to an integrity contract that distinguishes legitimate state changes from unauthorized substrate-level mutation, to the extent supported by the declared threat model.

The contract SHALL specify the trust anchors, integrity evidence, failure assumptions and conditions under which authenticity cannot be established.

Cryptographic hashes, authenticated logs, independent witnesses and hardware trust mechanisms MAY be used, but no particular mechanism is mandated by Phase 1.

A fully compromised trusted computing base may defeat these guarantees. Verification SHALL disclose that limitation.

───

### Evidence status and external assurance boundary

Specification, realization, observation, proof and certification are distinct. Candidate-authored logs and self-reports can be evidence inputs but do not alone establish independent Level N assurance. Document 1 specifies this distinction; the institutional structure, audit practice and certificate issuance protocol belong to later verification/evaluation work.

6.1 Multidimensional status

Capability assessment SHALL NOT use a single mutually exclusive status enumeration for declaration, availability, evidence and recoverability.

Each capability SHALL be evaluated through separate dimensions, including:

6.2 Developmental change

Developmental change SHALL be distinguished from routine configuration updates, ordinary learning and component replacement.

A developmental claim requires a declared transformation class and evidence that the change affects the organization's relevant internal structure, dispositions or operational capacities under the applicable contract.

This requirement does not prescribe a particular learning algorithm.

### Development and conformance

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-041, REQ-P1-048, REQ-P1-061 and REQ-P1-079.** Permitted developmental change does not automatically destroy conformance or numerical identity. A later certification contract may define a transformation envelope, but must assess cumulative as well as individual change and preserve role-critical obligations. The technical monitoring and reassessment algorithm is deferred.

7.1 Temporal distinctions

• Physical time.
• Logical or causal ordering.
• Observation time.

8. Existential continuity and cold suspension

8.1 Active minimal continuity

Active minimal continuity requires satisfaction of the applicable Phase 0 existential contract.

A running heartbeat alone SHALL NOT establish valid continuity if its relationship to protected existential state, governance authority or recovery contract cannot be demonstrated.

8.2 Dormancy

Dormancy denotes a governed active condition in which higher-level cognitive activity is unavailable or inactive while the required existential foundation remains operational.

Dormancy SHALL require evidence binding the active existential mechanism to the relevant governed instance and retained continuity-related state.

A disconnected heartbeat stub SHALL NOT establish dormancy.

8.3 Cold suspension

DEF-P1-029 — Cold Suspension

A governed lifecycle condition in which active execution, including the existential heartbeat, has ceased while designated state and continuity-related evidence are preserved under an authorized custody contract.

Cold suspension does not satisfy Phase 0's active operational-continuity condition.

8.4 Existential custody

REQ-P1-039 — Cold-Suspension Custody Contract

A claim of governed resumption following cold suspension SHALL require evidence of:

• Authorized suspension initiation.
• Valid pre-suspension lifecycle and governance state.
• Preserved state integrity.
• Custodial authority and custody history.
• Applicable exclusivity or fencing guarantees.
• Authorized reactivation.
• Absence of unresolved conflicting active continuations, or explicit disclosure of uncertainty.
• A continuity-adjudication result under the applicable rule.

A cryptographic custody lease MAY support this contract.

Such a lease SHALL NOT by itself establish numerical individual identity.

8.5 Termination and successor restoration

Definitive termination SHALL be adjudicated through an authorized lifecycle contract and supporting evidence.

Under the accepted Phase 0 convention, restoration from a retained backup after definitive termination creates a successor.

Phase 2 SHALL elaborate this distinction without silently reversing it.

───

### Provisional resumption and canonical custody

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-039, REQ-P1-060 and REQ-P1-078.** Authorized cold suspension and exclusive operational resumption establish neither numerical identity preservation nor termination by themselves. Until the applicable Phase 2 identity-adjudication contract has been applied, the identity outcome is **UNDETERMINED**. A provisional operational resumption may be governed under restricted authority only if pre-suspension canonical state and custody evidence cannot be overwritten: copy-on-write or an equivalent non-destructive lineage separation is required. Such a continuation cannot assert exclusive original identity or bootstrap new protected/ADMIN authority. This is a conceptual custody constraint; storage mechanics are deferred. Definitive termination followed by restoration remains successor restoration under Phase 0.

9. Continuity events, branching and replacement

9.1 Event taxonomy

The ontology SHALL represent pause, dormancy, cold suspension, resumption, migration, replacement, rollback, restoration in place, restoration after termination, branching, merging, partial loss and termination.

Events MAY be compound.

An event classification SHALL distinguish what occurred from what an observer believes occurred.

9.2 Continuity admissibility

REQ-P1-036 — Continuity-Event Evidence Schema

Every continuity-relevant event SHALL identify its pre-event instance referent, event class, authority, affected state, lifecycle outcome, lineage relation, evidence provenance and adjudication status.

REQ-P1-040 — Rule-Relative Continuity Admissibility

Every continuity claim SHALL identify the rule under which it is evaluated.

Until the applicable Phase 2 contract is accepted, unresolved claims SHALL remain pending rather than defaulting to identity preservation.

The accepted post-termination successor convention remains binding.

9.3 Diachronic cohesion

REQ-P1-041 — Transformation Cohesion Parameters

Continuity contracts SHALL account for the possibility that a succession of individually authorized modifications may collectively alter the organization beyond the permitted transformation class.

The contract SHALL identify relevant change magnitude, change rate, role criticality, dependency preservation and organizational integration conditions.

No universal numerical replacement threshold is prescribed by Phase 1.

10. Partitioning and distributed realization

10.1 Partitioned constituents

REQ-P1-042 — Partition and Existential Authority

An involuntary partition SHALL NOT automatically create multiple valid NNNC instances.

Authoritative existential operation SHALL be evaluated against the accepted fencing, governance and lifecycle contract.

A partition that lacks valid existential authority may remain an unreachable or orphaned constituent group without acquiring independent entity identity.

10.2 Split-brain protection

If multiple partitions claim exclusive existential authority, the system SHALL either identify one authoritative claimant under the accepted contract or classify the situation as unresolved.

It SHALL NOT certify incompatible simultaneous exclusive-continuation claims.

The contract SHALL address stale leases, delayed messages, clock uncertainty, recovery races and competing governance histories.

───

### Partition and protected-effect safety boundary

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-052, REQ-P1-053, REQ-P1-057, REQ-P1-058 and REQ-P1-076.** A minority or stale partition does not acquire exclusive protected authority by asserting necessity or survival. When a valid exclusive-authority dispute exists, unsafe protected effects remain fenced; liveness cannot override authorization. Pre-authorized containment may be permitted without claiming identity preservation or successful recovery. Safe-hold is a functional governance mode, not a newly invented lifecycle class. Resource-side fencing, delayed command handling, recovery deadlines and fault-model proofs remain Phase 0 engineering / later integration obligations, not requirements to implement within Document 1.

11. Neutrality, endogenous regulation and agency

11.1 Architectural neutrality

Neutrality SHALL not mean the absence of goals, tasks, preferences or environmental interaction.

For the foundational architecture, neutrality means that constitutive organizational qualification is not defined by a single externally assigned task objective.

The specification SHALL permit task substitution without requiring redefinition of the entity's foundational ontology.

11.2 Endogenous activity

A system does not demonstrate endogenous regulation merely by executing periodically or responding to an internally stored flag.

An evaluation SHALL examine whether internal conditions causally influence the initiation, selection, inhibition or prioritization of permitted transitions.

This is an architectural criterion, not a claim of free will or consciousness.

11.3 Experimental status

HYP-P1-001

A realized NNNC architecture combining persistent existential governance, internal cognitive integration, endogenous regulation and developmental plasticity may exhibit forms of autonomous organization not adequately characterized by ordinary request-response task execution.

This remains a research hypothesis.

───

### Neutrality is not a scheduler flag

**Consolidated interpretation of REQ-P1-049.** Endogenous regulation requires an internal-state-dependent, causally attributable ability to initiate, inhibit, select or reprioritize activity; an externally fixed task plus a cron trigger or cosmetic state flag does not suffice. Exact experimental criteria and objective-flexibility tests are deferred to their authoritative domain/evaluation contracts.

12. Epistemic safeguards and non-claims

12.1 Symmetric epistemic restraint

EPI-P1-007 — Symmetric Non-Inference

Architectural conformance, operational activity, behavioral sophistication and self-report SHALL NOT by themselves establish either the presence or absence of phenomenal consciousness, subjective experience or moral status.

A lack of evidence SHALL NOT automatically be treated as evidence of absence.

12.2 Additional non-claims

This specification does not establish:

• That organizational continuity is phenomenal continuity.
• That substrate abstraction means physical substrate independence.
• That endogenous regulation grants unrestricted authority.
• That the ontology is a complete metaphysical account of existence.
• That formal models are already verified.
• That conceptual conformance establishes operational reliability.
• That architectural integration proves general intelligence.
• That any Systemic Algorithm has been implemented merely because its name appears in a component manifest.

12.3 Architectural incompleteness is not permission for substitution

The present absence of a complete authoritative specification for an architectural component SHALL NOT be interpreted as evidence that the component is equivalent to an arbitrary conventional mechanism.

Nor SHALL that absence be interpreted as proof that no conventional mechanism could contribute to its eventual realization.

Claims of equivalence, partial realization and complete realization SHALL be evaluated against the relevant accepted contracts when those contracts become available.

13. Mathematical formalization handoff

Document 2 SHALL define typed sets and relations for referents, constitutive specifications, organizational instances, constituents, boundaries, states, processes, events, transitions, governance authority, temporal qualification, continuity claims, lineage, profiles and evidence.

The mathematical handoff SHALL distinguish: (a) computational-entity qualification; (b) architectural-candidate/reference-kernel status; (c) Level N architectural eligibility; (d) empirical conformance evidence; and (e) authorization of a public certification claim. None is inferred from another without its declared conditions.

Let **D_floor** denote the fixed, project-adopted set of mandatory architectural domains, including Phase 0 existential foundations, endogenous regulation, organizational integration, memory, all five FFNN flows, all six Systemic Algorithms, development, governance, and environment relationships. A profile MAY add requirements but SHALL NOT remove members of D_floor. The five flows and six algorithms require distinct accepted realization contracts; their names alone do not count.

Document 2 SHALL express Level N eligibility as a necessary conjunction of accepted and adequate mandatory-domain contracts, evidence of realized satisfaction, system-wide integration, Phase 0 obligations applicable to the claimed operational scope, and a suitable evaluation context. Eligibility is **not** certificate issuance: an authorized Level N claim additionally requires the applicable evaluation and certificate decision. An incomplete authoritative mandatory contract yields **NOT YET CERTIFIABLE**, not proof of nonconformance.

No formula in Document 1 is a proven theorem, executable verifier, or sufficient certification algorithm. The formal semantics and test oracles belong to Document 2.

# ANNEX B — Foundational v1.1 definitions retained for conceptual reference

These definitions are recovered from the supplied v1.1 text. Where an earlier definition and the consolidated body differ (especially cold suspension and qualification), **the consolidated body is operative**. No historical authentication claim is made.

DEF-P1-001 — Ontological universe
The foundational universe is a domain of distinguishable referents, occurrences, descriptions and relationships available to the specification.
It is a modeling domain, not a claim that all existing things are computationally observable.

DEF-P1-002 — Organizational boundary
An organizational boundary is a governed partition over a specified domain of referents, states, processes and interfaces.
The partition identifies what is included in an organization, what remains external, and which interactions or transitions may cross the partition.
A boundary is defined by its governing specification and applicable authoritative state, not by the prior assumption that an entity already exists.

DEF-P1-003 — Computational entity
A computational entity is a bounded, state-bearing organizational unit whose constituent processes and states participate in a governed organization under a specified operational and temporal contract.
This definition describes a broad class of computational systems.
It does not establish NNNC qualification.

DEF-P1-004 — Endogenous regulation
Endogenous regulation is the capacity of an organization to initiate, regulate, inhibit or prioritize permitted internal transitions on the basis of its internal conditions, rather than operating exclusively as a direct response to externally supplied commands.

DEF-P1-005 — Developmental plasticity
Developmental plasticity is the capacity for governed changes to internal representations, relationships, mechanisms or organizational configuration across time.

DEF-P1-006 — Organizational unity
Organizational unity is the condition in which constituent roles, state, processes, interfaces and governance relationships form a specified coordinated organization under a common constitutive contract.
Organizational unity is not established merely by shared storage, common process ownership, co-location or a common identifier.

DEF-P1-007 — Decisional dependence
Decisional dependence is a relation describing the extent to which a specified decision or outcome depends upon a particular constituent or external service.

DEF-P1-008 — Realization relation
A realization relation associates an organizational mechanism or state with the computational resources and substrate structures that implement it.
The relation does not establish identity between the organization and its substrate.

DEF-P1-009 — Capability
A capability is a disposition to produce specified effects under stated conditions, resources, interventions and evaluation assumptions.
A capability is distinct from an instance of its exercise.

DEF-P1-010 — State
State is a temporally qualified configuration of properties or state variables associated with a specified organizational model.
State is not equivalent to the entity possessing it.

DEF-P1-011 — Governed transition
A governed transition is an admissible change in an organizational configuration under applicable preconditions, authority, ordering and consistency rules.
A difference between two observed snapshots is not sufficient proof that a valid transition occurred.

DEF-P1-012 — Operational persistence
Operational persistence is the maintenance of the applicable organizational and continuity requirements across a specified interval under a declared continuity rule.
Persistence SHALL NOT be inferred solely from:
• A process identifier.
• A database identifier.
• A checkpoint.
• A heartbeat observation.
• A self-report.
• Similar behavior.
• Identical stored information.

DEF-P1-013 — Governed dormancy
Dormancy is an operational condition in which higher-level cognitive activity is inactive or unavailable while the minimum existential operation required by the applicable Phase 0 contract remains active.
Dormancy does not establish subjective continuity.

DEF-P1-014 — Cold suspension
Cold suspension is a governed condition in which active execution has ceased and preserved state may support later reactivation.
Cold suspension is not equivalent to active minimal operation.
Whether a later reactivation preserves numerical identity is determined by the applicable continuity contract, not by the existence of a saved state.

DEF-P1-015 — Qualification
Qualification is satisfaction of the applicable architectural criteria.

DEF-P1-016 — Recognition
Recognition is an observer's evidence-based classification of whether those criteria are satisfied.
An entity may qualify without being recognized.
An observer may recognize a candidate incorrectly.

DEF-P1-017 — Interface
An interface is a governed interaction point through which specified information, events or effects may cross an organizational boundary.
An interface does not automatically incorporate the external participant into the organization.

DEF-P1-018 — Architectural neutrality
Architectural neutrality means that NNNC's fundamental identity and organization are not defined exclusively by one externally imposed task, fixed reward objective or permanent environment.
Neutrality does not imply an absence of preferences, internal goals, evaluative criteria or motivations.
Architectural neutrality is distinct from epistemic neutrality concerning consciousness claims.

# Part II — Operative v2.3.1 amendments

## CU.0 Authority, scope and interpretation

This is a substantive, bounded revision of the retained v2.0 text. It makes the existing DEF-P1-006 organizational-unity definition and §3 integration commitments assessable through an explicit constitutive-organization contract. It does not introduce a claim that unity was previously absent from the architecture. These amendments control only where expressly stated; all other retained commitments remain operative. Historical acceptance and verification statements in Part I describe their source revision. This candidate has not been adopted.

The governing Phase 0 reference pair is now **NNNC-P0-D1 v1.4.2 and NNNC-P0-D2 v1.4, conditionally adopted 23 October 2026**. Conditional adoption of that pair supplies architectural contracts; it does not discharge P0-O01–08 or imply production enforcement. The recovered unamended P0-D2 v1.3 source has been reconciled in that pair. Earlier missing exact registers identified by that reconciliation remain open; this revision does not invent them.

**DEF-P1-CU-01 — Constitutive unity.** At a declared scope, mode and interval, an organization has constitutive unity when its applicable constituent roles, state and processes realize the governing constitutive specification through the required coordinated relationships and governed boundary. Realization requires actual participation in the specified organizational processing, rather than common names, custody, administration, communication volume or independent local activity. This refines DEF-P1-006; it does not replace cognitive unity, numerical identity or certification.

Constitutive unity is relative to a specified organizational contract, not a claim that every physical part must influence every other part. Modular, distributed, hierarchical, redundant and replaceable implementations are permitted when they realize the applicable relationships. A generic Level E organization need not realize cognition. A Level N claim additionally retains the complete mandatory floor, internal cognitive integration, endogenous regulation, accepted domain contracts and evidence requirements. A Level N claim SHALL establish constitutive unity relative to an applicable versioned constitutive-organization contract, in addition to all retained qualification requirements. Unity is necessary and never sufficient for Level N. This explicitly supplements DEF-P1-024 and REQ-P1-030; D2 may formalize this requirement but may not weaken the floor.


**DEF-P1-CU-02 — Specified organizational processing.** The governing contract's required state transformations, role interactions, regulation and readiness conditions, including their input/output and state interfaces and their operating contexts. This processing is prescribed independently of the candidate's success. It includes the behavior required of an idle or waiting mechanism; it does not demand continuous processor activity.

**DEF-P1-CU-03 — Required composition.** Joint realization of all applicable roles and coordinated relationships specified for that processing, under their governing boundary and state/process contracts. Components communicating or sharing a store do not suffice unless the required relationships actually hold.

**DEF-P1-CU-04 — Context-dependent contribution.** A constituent or permitted joint configuration makes the contractually specified causal contribution under the declared relevant state/input, operating or readiness conditions. Different contexts may use different acceptable witnesses. The applicable contexts come from the governing contract, never from choosing only successful trials. Redundant alternatives may each preserve the required relation; a jointly sufficient configuration need not have individually sufficient members.

These definitions refine DEF-P1-006 and supply the organizational composition underlying REQ-P1-032. They neither replace its NNNC-specific cognitive integration tests nor reduce cognitive unity to generic functional coordination. D2's unity definition is explicitly relative to a supplied interpretation; incomplete later-domain contracts remain missing certification prerequisites.

## CU.1 New normative requirements

**P1-CU-01 — Versioned organization contract.** A claim of constitutive unity SHALL identify a versioned constitutive-organization contract bound to the candidate, implementation/configuration, governing specification, profile, mode, scope and interval. It SHALL identify applicable roles and organizational relationships, their state/process interfaces and completion or participation conditions, membership and boundary rules, relevant external dependencies, governance and permitted changes, and the evidence and discriminating tests needed for the claim. The contract supplements the existing role and domain contracts; it cannot replace an unfinished FFNN or Systemic Algorithm definition.

**P1-CU-02 — Independent applicability and completeness.** Applicable roles and relationships SHALL be derived from the governing specification, profile, mode and scope independently of successful realization and independently of the candidate's submitted witness. An omitted, malformed or selectively narrow contract SHALL NOT remove a mandatory obligation. Operational-unity claims SHALL include at least one substantive role and coordinated relationship; trivial labels or periodic logging cannot satisfy this condition. A prototype MAY report a bounded partial result, provided that it does not represent the omitted mandatory organization as realized or issue a complete Level N claim.

**P1-CU-03 — Role and state realization.** Each applicable role SHALL have an actual realizing constituent (including a governed composite constituent permitted by the applicable role contract) bound to the candidate under the applicable membership rules. Its specified state/process obligations SHALL hold in the declared interval. Registration, a manifest, available source code, passing an isolated component test or a service signature alone SHALL NOT establish that realization. State and process interfaces SHALL resolve to the same candidate and configuration as the claim.

**P1-CU-04 — Consequential composition.** Each applicable organizational relationship SHALL be realized by the required participating roles and state/process interactions in the claimed organizational processing. Pairwise interaction or genuine but irrelevant local activity SHALL NOT establish the whole contract. Verification SHALL distinguish a connected or cooperating collection from the required composition. It SHALL permit joint sufficiency, redundancy and context-dependent contribution; failure of single-component removal to change an output is not, by itself, proof of non-contribution. No universal central controller, all-to-all connectivity or single physical execution process is required.

**P1-CU-05 — Boundary and support separation.** Internal constituent membership, external resource dependence, custody, control and trust SHALL remain distinct. Phase 0 NES support contracts SHALL supply the substrate assumptions for any dependent progress claim; a provider's promise alone SHALL NOT establish actual or independently justified support. Loss of support SHALL NOT erase an otherwise applicable end-to-end obligation, establish a mechanism breach without attribution evidence, establish termination or identity preservation, or create authority, budget or shutdown exemptions. An observer unable to run during total support loss need not fabricate an immediate record; later assessment SHALL report the observation gap and available evidence honestly.

**P1-CU-06 — Governed transformation and reassessment.** Membership, contract, mode and implementation changes SHALL use the existing authorization rules and preserve prior version bindings and historical assessments. A change affecting a required role, relationship, assumption or evidence binding SHALL trigger scoped reassessment, including cumulative change under the existing developmental-envelope requirement. A new contract SHALL NOT rewrite an earlier unmet obligation into a met one. Corrections and reassessments SHALL preserve the prior assessment records and append a linked new assessment identifying the applicable old and new versions. This constrains the logical audit history; it does not forbid authorized changes to live operational state or lawful correction of an erroneous verdict through a retained, traceable new record. Reassessment neither proves nor disproves numerical identity; Phase 2 governs that question.

**P1-CU-07 — Truth, evidence and qualification.** Constitutive unity under the applicable contract is necessary for every Level N claim, cumulatively with DEF-P1-024, REQ-P1-030–032 and REQ-P1-044; it is never sufficient. Actual constitutive unity, a well-formed declared contract, an evidence-supported assessment, certification eligibility and an authorized certification claim SHALL remain distinct. The D2 open-world decision policy applies: demonstrated mandatory violation takes precedence as FAIL; otherwise absent authoritative mandatory contracts yield NOT_YET_CERTIFIABLE; otherwise insufficient evidence yields UNDETERMINED. PASS requires complete positive support within the stated scope. None of these outcomes may be substituted for a philosophical or empirical claim outside that scope.

**P1-CU-08 — Verification handoff.** Document 2 SHALL define a typed native-model interpretation, independent applicability, role and relationship witnesses, a truth/evidence separation and a property-level verification ledger. It SHALL include a constructive finite witness, a similarly constituted disconnected negative control, external-wrapper and irrelevant-local-activity controls, redundancy and evidence controls, and a Phase 0 dependency check.
